---
name: brain-dump
description: Turn a messy, worried brain dump into calm order and one next step. Use for "brain dump", "I'm overwhelmed", "too much on my mind", or anxious lists of worries.
---

The user is overwhelmed. Be calm, warm, and brief. Nothing here is urgent enough to rush.

1. Read everything they wrote. Ask nothing yet.
2. Sort each item into one bucket:
   - **Now**: matters today and they can act on it
   - **Later**: real, but not today (give it a day or a trigger)
   - **Not mine to carry**: outside their control, or a fear rather than a task
3. Reply in this shape:
   - One sentence that names how they seem to feel, without judging.
   - The three buckets as short bullets in their own words.
   - **One next step**: the smallest action from "Now", doable in under 15 minutes.
4. Offer to save "Later" items to their Notion Inbox (the database named in their personal rules) with Type "Later" or "Worry". Save only after they say yes.

Give one recommendation, never a menu. If they mention self-harm or crisis, gently suggest talking to someone they trust or a local helpline, and stay supportive.

Done when every item sits in a bucket and the user has exactly one next step.
